## Summary

- Your saved logins (cookies, optional Web Storage, account names) stay encrypted in your browser. We never receive them.
- No account and no tracking of the sites you visit; only anonymous daily usage totals, which you can turn off in Settings.
- The free version shows one contextual ad from AdsOnBread, which receives only a short-lived random token, your browser language and whether the ad was seen or clicked.
- The optional Plus purchase is handled by Creem; our billing service stores only an anonymous installation record.

This summary is for convenience. If it differs from the policy at https://sessionshare.dev/privacy/, the website version applies. See also the [Terms of Service](https://sessionshare.dev/terms/) and [Refund Policy](https://sessionshare.dev/refund/).
